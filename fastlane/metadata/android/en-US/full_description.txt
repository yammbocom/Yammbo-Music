## 🎵 Yammbo Music

Yammbo Music is a free, open-source music and podcast player. Stream songs, videos and
podcasts, download them for offline listening, and enjoy a clean, distraction-free
interface built for people who love music.

This F-Droid / IzzyOnDroid build ships with no ads, no trackers and no proprietary
Google services.

---

## ⚠️ Note

- Online content is played through the official YouTube IFrame Player API inside a WebView
  (this is why the app is flagged with the NonFreeNet anti-feature).
- Your data stays on your device.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎧 Listening
- Songs, albums, artists, videos, playlists and podcasts
- Offline downloads
- Synced lyrics (fetch, display, translate)
- Listening statistics and "your year in music"

### 🎙️ Podcasts
- Popular and recommended podcasts on the Home screen
- Player controls made for long episodes: variable speed and skip forward / back

### ⏰ Extras
- Sleep timer
- Fast Share
- Android Auto and Android TV support (experimental)
- Home widgets (experimental)
